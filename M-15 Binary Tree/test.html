How to Connect Your Google Sheets

Step 1: Prepare Your Google Sheet

  • Create a Google Sheet with these columns: Name, Blood Group, Phone, Location, Last Donation (YYYY-MM-DD), Donations Count, Status (Available/Not Available)
  • Make sure the sheet has a header row (first row with column names)
  • Keep the data organized and consistent

Step 2: Publish Your Sheet

  • Go to File → Share → Publish to web
  • Choose "Entire Document" and "Web page" format
  • Click "Publish" and copy the URL
  • Paste the URL in the box below and click Connect
